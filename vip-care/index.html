02 · THE PROBLEM
Structural, not cyclical

Senior care in Central Europe is structurally broken.

  • 01

    Acute staff shortage

    Slovakia is short 14,000 nurses; the median age in care is 51. Existing facilities cannot scale because they cannot hire.

  • 02

    OPEX trapped in personnel

    Traditional facilities run 60–65% of operating cost on labor and another 15–22% on energy — leaving no capital for quality, technology, or growth.

  • 03

    DACH demand has nowhere to go

    Austrian and German seniors face waitlists of 18+ months and prices of €5,400–8,800 per month, with no nearby quality alternatives.

The market is asking for a different operating model — not just more beds.

03Our solution
A patented operating model

A senior care platform built as technology, with operations as the application layer.

Five integrated layers replace the labor-heavy model. Combined effect per facility: −24 FTE (50% reduction), −56% energy, and +18–20% wages for remaining staff.
